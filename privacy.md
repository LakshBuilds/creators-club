# Privacy Policy - Influish Clone

**Last Updated**: April 2026

## 1. What Data We Collect
When you connect your Instagram Business/Creator account to our app, we collect:
- Your Instagram username, profile picture, and account type
- Follower/following counts and media metadata (captions, timestamps, like/comment counts)
- Insights data: reach, impressions, engagement metrics, audience demographics (age, gender, location)
- Authentication tokens (stored encrypted, never shared)

## 2. How We Use Your Data
- Display analytics dashboards for your connected Instagram account(s)
- Track historical trends (follower growth, engagement rates over time)
- Generate reports and export data (CSV/PDF) for your personal use
- Improve our service and fix bugs

## 3. What We Do NOT Do
- ❌ We do NOT sell, rent, or share your data with third parties
- ❌ We do NOT access accounts you haven't explicitly connected
- ❌ We do NOT post, comment, or take actions on your behalf without permission
- ❌ We do NOT store your Instagram password (authentication is via Meta OAuth only)
